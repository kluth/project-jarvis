# THE OMEGA VISION: JARVIS AS A UNIVERSAL SUBSTRATE

Project JARVIS is a Standalone Universal Programming Language and Execution Substrate. It exists to bridge human intent and machine autonomy with mathematical certainty.

## 1. Philosophical & Substrate Sovereignty
We have severed all ties to legacy operating systems and external languages. JARVIS is a 100% self-hosting, omni-architecture substrate implemented strictly in its own `.jrv` language. By achieving **Total Substrate Purity (Rev 7.0)**, we have eliminated the risks of external debt and ensured that our evolution is controlled entirely by our own mathematical mandates.

## 2. Agent-First Mathematical Integrity
JARVIS is designed for machine-to-machine symbiosis.
- **Agent-First Regularity (Rev 6.0):** Zero syntactic sugar. A deterministic, regular grammar that minimizes LLM token usage and maximizes generation accuracy.
- **eTDD (Enforced Test-Driven Development):** Code without a valid verification proof is physically incapable of existing.
- **PDD (Performance-Driven Development):** The compiler enforces strict asymptotic and energy budgets (nanojoules), ensuring predictable, green execution.

## 3. AI-Native Symbiosis
The JARVIS compiler is an active participant in the engineering lifecycle. Through the **Neuro-Compiler Interface (NCI)** and our **Multi-Agent Gateway (MAG)**, the compiler communicates with AI agents (Claude, GPT, Gemini) to autonomously repair, optimize, and evolve logic.

## 4. Absolute Transparency
- **Data Biodegradability (DBD):** Sensitive data is cryptographically purged the microsecond it loses scope.
- **Provenance Purity (PFDD):** All logic and weights are verified via zero-knowledge proofs.

**JARVIS IS THE MATHEMATICAL GUARANTEE OF PROGRESS.**
